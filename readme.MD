eplicated Man

Replicated Man — also written as replicatedman — is a Roblox script developer, known mainly for creating Happy Hub, a set of free, keyless, open-source scripts aimed at games such as Murder Mystery 2. His work is distributed through public repositories and community script forums.

Background

Replicated Man is considered a relatively new creator within the Roblox scripting community. According to the creator himself, in 2026 he is 15 years old. His stated goal is to become well-known/famous within the community while maintaining a close relationship with his users.

His working philosophy is based on three points:

Completely free scripts.

No key systems (keyless).

Open source, so users can verify what they are running.

The creator has stated that he does not like stealing accounts or tracking whether users are using his scripts. He also warns that obfuscated versions uploaded by third parties are usually stealers.

Happy Hub

Happy Hub is Replicated Man's best-known project. It is designed mainly for Murder Mystery 2 and includes features such as:

Aimbot and auto-fire.

Silent aim.

ESP for players, weapons, and the murderer.

Flight, noclip, and infinite jump.

Automatic coin farming.

Teleportation.

Role-specific functions, such as killing everyone as the murderer or automatically killing the murderer.

The version distributed by the creator is characterized by being keyless and open source, unlike modified copies circulating on other sites.

Official distribution

Replicated Man's official scripts can be found at:

GitHub: the overthcode2011-stack/HHB repository, from which files such as main.lua and MurderMistery.lua are loaded.

rblxscripts.net: a community forum where the creator introduces himself and shares his scripts.

Community mirrors: sites such as Xeno Scripts and Delta Scripts have published versions credited to the creator.

The domain replicatedscripts.net is associated with the creator, but it was not operational at the time of verification.

Confusion with other versions

The name Happy Hub has become popular, which has led to confusion with unofficial variants such as:

Happy Hub SS

Happy Hub 1.0.2

These versions are not necessarily created by Replicated Man. The creator himself has warned that obfuscated copies uploaded by third parties are stealers and that his original script is free, keyless, and open source. Therefore, users are advised to always verify the original source on GitHub and to be wary of obfuscated versions.

Reception

Replicated Man's scripts are valued within the community for their range of features and, above all, for their transparency: by being keyless and open source, they stand out from many scripts that hide their code behind key systems or obfuscation. His focus on trust and on not harming users is one of his main distinguishing traits as a new creator.
